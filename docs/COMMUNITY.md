# EGO TOOL Community Guidelines

These rules apply everywhere the EGO TOOL community meets: GitHub, our Discord server and any
official event or challenge.

## Our goal
A friendly place where people help each other focus, build better habits and improve EGO TOOL.
Progress, not perfection.

## The rules

1. **Be kind.** No insults, harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.
2. **No shaming.** Never mock someone's streak, struggles, or progress. Everyone starts somewhere.
3. **Stay on topic.** Use the right channel. Off-topic chat goes in the off-topic channel.
4. **No spam or self-promotion.** No ads, referral links, or DM advertising. Sharing your own
   productivity content is allowed once a week in the showcase channel.
5. **Respect privacy.** Don't share anyone's personal information, screenshots of private
   conversations, or Pod members' data.
6. **No medical advice.** Habit and focus tips are welcome, but we are not doctors. For health
   issues, talk to a professional.
7. **English in main channels.** Use the language channels for other languages.
8. **Report, don't retaliate.** If something's wrong, contact a moderator instead of arguing.
9. **Keep it safe for work.** No NSFW content, anywhere.
10. **Follow Discord's Terms of Service** and community guidelines.

## Enforcement

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Friendly reminder from a moderator |
| 2 | Formal warning |
| 3 | 24-hour timeout |
| 4 | Ban |

Serious violations (hate speech, doxxing, threats) lead to an immediate ban.

## Roles

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| **Founder** | Project founder |
| **Core Team** | Design and app developers |
| **Moderator** | Volunteers who keep the community safe |
| **Beta Tester** | Members testing early builds and Pods |
| **Translator** | Community translators |
| **Streak Master** | Members with a 100+ day streak (shared in #wins) |
| **Member** | Everyone who accepted the rules |

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday** — Weekly Focus Challenge starts (post your goal)
- **Wednesday** — Mid-week check-in thread
- **Friday** — Wins Friday (share what went well)
- **Sunday** — Weekly Mirror share (optional)

Moderators can be reached in the support channel or by mentioning **@Moderator**.
