# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is EGO TOOL?**
A free focus timer and habit tracker. You plan focus sessions, track daily habits, and every
week the *Weekly Mirror* tells you honestly how it went.

**Why is it called EGO TOOL?**
Because the hardest person to keep promises to is yourself. EGO TOOL helps you check your ego:
did you actually do what you said you would? (The *Ego Check* feature is where the name came from.)

**Is it really free?**
Yes. EGO TOOL is MIT-licensed and free forever. EGO Sync (encrypted cloud sync) is free up to
3 devices. We may add an optional paid "Supporter" tier later — no features will ever be removed
from the free version.

**Do I need an account?**
No. An account is only needed for EGO Sync and Accountability Pods.

## Privacy & data

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device by default. If you turn on EGO Sync, your data is end-to-end encrypted before it
leaves your device — we can't read it.

**Can my Pod see my habits?**
Pod members only see your **streak numbers and whether you checked in today**. They never see
habit names, notes, focus details or your Ego Check answers.

**How do I export or delete my data?**
Settings → Data → *Export (JSON/CSV)* or *Delete everything*. Deleting your account also deletes
all synced data within 30 days.

## Features

**What's a Streak Shield?**
Every 7-day streak earns one shield (max 3). If you miss a day, a shield is used automatically and
your streak survives.

**What's a "minimum version" habit?**
A tiny version of a habit you can always do, even on bad days (e.g. *1 push-up* instead of *30*).
Doing the minimum still counts for your streak.

**Can I block websites during focus sessions?**
On Windows, macOS and Linux, yes (Settings → Focus → Blocklist). Not available on Android yet.

**What are Accountability Pods?**
Small groups of 3–5 people who keep each other on track. Currently in **beta** — join the beta via
our community.

## Troubleshooting

**The timer stops when my phone screen is off (Android).**
Allow EGO TOOL to run in the background: Settings → Apps → EGO TOOL → Battery → *Unrestricted*.

**My streak reset even though I had a shield.**
Shields apply at midnight in your device's time zone. If you travel, check Settings → Time zone.
If it still looks wrong, report a bug with the template.

**Sync is stuck on "Waiting…".**
Sign out and back in on one device, then open the app on the other device. If it continues,
open a bug report and include your app version.

**How do I report a bug?**
Use the [bug report template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md) on GitHub Issues.
